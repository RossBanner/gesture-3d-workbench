# Gesture 3D Workbench 1.2 — quick controls

Save your current work, close the old window and reopen **Gesture 3D Workbench** from the Desktop shortcut. Click **Start Camera**.

**Choose a hand tool without a keyboard:** point at its button in **HAND TOOLS**, pinch, open your fingers, then pinch a shape.

- **Move:** one pinch moves the shape. Two pinches spread/squeeze to resize, or turn like a steering wheel to roll.
- **Depth Z:** pinch a shape and slide up/down. Down increases Z; up decreases Z. X/Y, rotation and size stay locked. Look for the blue guide, starting outline and live Z value.
- **Rotate:** pinch and move sideways to turn, or up/down to tilt. A second pinch can steer by moving relative to the first. You do not need to tilt your palm. Position and size stay locked.
- **Size:** pinch and move up/down, or use two pinches and spread/squeeze. Position stays locked.
- **Optional fist shortcut:** in Move, hold one pinch and close the other hand into a fist. Once **Fist locked** appears, slide the grabbing hand up/down for Z. Open the helper palm for about a quarter second to unlock it.
- **Release:** open your grabbing fingers to place. A brief detection gap holds the object still; after a longer loss, open and re-grab.
- **Hand menu:** add, colour, undo, save/load local scenes, change views and stop the camera.
- **View:** pinch empty space to orbit; add another pinch and spread/squeeze to zoom.

Estimated push/pull starts **off**. You can enable it in Hand menu, but it remains a palm-size estimate. **Depth Z** provides deliberate scene movement. Perspective can alter apparent screen size during depth movement; the object's Scale values remain unchanged.

New controls passed recorded/simulated regression checks; a live physical two-hand session still needs your trial.
